Create a concept review image for Skyttel, a personal spatial household map. This is a design sketch, not an implemented screenshot. A large high-resolution 2x2 comparison sheet of THE SAME spatial scene in four backgrounds: top left dark plain deep green, top right dark cosmic mist and sparse stars, bottom left light plain off-white, bottom right light ocean softly patterned pale blue. Each panel has ample room, exact headings "Dark / plain", "Dark / cosmic", "Light / plain", "Light / ocean". Minimal surrounding UI, no invented navigation controls.
CRITICAL GEOMETRY: three FULL square planes, mutually perpendicular in 3D, each CENTERED on the SAME origin (0,0,0); their intersection is through their centers, NOT three faces meeting at a corner. Show an oblique orthographic view with y vertical, x diagonally down-right and z diagonally down-left. Each square extends equally in both directions on both its axes and projects as a parallelogram/rhombus. Horizontal plane XZ has y=0, vertical plane YZ has x=0, vertical plane XY has z=0. All three remain visible, no floor/wall/room metaphor, no physical measurement ticks. Draw all three planes through one hollow origin ring. Three subtle axis lines crossing at the ring.
Visual proposal: low-opacity translucent fills, sparse grid of only 4x4 cells each, stronger outer borders, distinct patterns plus exact label per plane: teal solid border "y = 0 / XZ", amber dashed border "x = 0 / YZ", violet dotted border "z = 0 / XY". Direct labels on opaque theme-matching small backing for readability, never rely on color alone. Origin label "0,0,0". Avoid intensely luminous overlapping surfaces. All three surfaces should remain individually traceable.
Place six household objects around and within the planes, above and below and on both signed sides of each coordinate plane. Each object is a small circular flat line-icon badge plus an opaque readable short text label. Names: "Alex" person icon, "Lo" person icon, "Home" house icon, "Car" car icon, "Music" star icon, "Bank" bank icon. Connect Alex to Home, Lo to Car, Alex to Music, Bank to Home using thin readable curved arrowed relationship lines. Add small relationship labels "uses", "uses", "uses", "pays". Keep the same object positions and camera in all four panels. Protect object labels and relationship labels from plane grids. Objects and relationships should be the strongest content; planes secondary; decorative backgrounds tertiary.
Small footer "Concept for review · fixed world origin · no physical scale". Clean restrained functional interface concept, accurate coordinate geometry over visual drama. No extra planes, no perspective floor underneath, no 3D room, no floating dashboard cards.
